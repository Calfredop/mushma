title: A CC BY-NC regional forest map is acceptable when no open one exists
status: Done
---
When a region's only usable forest or land-use map is CC BY-NC, ask the human rather than falling back to CLC IV silently; for Abruzzo (2026-09-28) they accepted the CC BY-NC map because the app is non-commercial, credited with its licence, and the region doc says a commercial use would need CLC IV.

Why: the region cards say "CC BY, IODL 2.0 or equivalent", else CLC IV alone, but CLC IV missed 25 % of Abruzzo's forest against INFC while the CC BY-NC regional map was within 2 %.
